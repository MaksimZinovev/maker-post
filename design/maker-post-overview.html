Step by step

From photo to post

The happy path — Andrei's daily flow. Under 30 seconds of his time.

1
Andrei · iPhone

Take a photo

Opens the native camera app and snaps a photo of his latest 3D print. Nothing special — just a rough photo.

2
Andrei · Photos app

Select & share

Opens Photos, selects the photo, taps the Share button. Standard iOS flow — no new app to learn.

3
Andrei · Share sheet

Tap "Maker Post"

Taps the Maker Post action in the share sheet. The iOS Shortcut uploads the photo to the Worker and opens the review page.

4
Worker · AI pipeline

AI generates a draft

Stage 1: a vision model describes the photo factually. Stage 2: an LLM writes a caption in Andrei's voice, using his style guide and past approved posts as reference. Takes ~3–5 seconds.

5
Andrei · Web review

Review the draft

Sees a single card: photo on top, AI-generated caption below. Reads it. This is where he spends his 10 seconds.

6
Andrei · One-tap tweaks

Tweak (optional)

If the caption isn't quite right, taps a one-tap tweak: "shorter", "more casual", "add hashtags". Each tap updates his style guide for next time. Or skips this step entirely.

✓
Andrei · Web review

Approve & Post

Taps "Approve & Post". The Worker publishes to Instagram via the Graph API. Done. The approved post joins the RAG library for future drafts.
